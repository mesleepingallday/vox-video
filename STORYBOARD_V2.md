# Storyboard v2: chapters 1–2 (sentences 0–65)

Principles (from the owner's feedback, 4 Oct 2026):
- No chapter title screens. Chapters change with a match cut.
- Show, don't label: real people as photos (photo cutout on first appearance, redrawn afterwards), brands as their logos,
  products as their interfaces, ideas as objects or metaphors. Text lives on things in the scene (calendar, cheque,
  address bar, marquee, clipboard, newspaper), not on floating cards.
- Paper-collage identity kept, pushed further: halftone photos, cut-paper puppets with jointed limbs, Win98-era UI
  recreations, physical props, camera moves through a continuous desk/world, motion on twos where hand-made.
- Seedance is not used for new footage; the founders test clip is a motion/quality reference (and rotoscope input if
  the host is opened).
